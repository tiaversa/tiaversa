About Me

Hi, I'm Timna! I turn messy data into pipelines people can trust, and I like explaining how it all works while I'm at it.

What I do

I'm a data engineer based in Berlin. I build and maintain ETL pipelines, model data, and make sure the numbers people rely on are the right ones. I've modernised legacy infrastructure, consolidated data warehouses in Snowflake and brought dbt into pipelines so they're more reliable and reusable. I know my way around AWS, Azure and GCP, and I've worked with Databricks.

  • Open to collaborating on beginner-friendly open source

Outside of work

Photography, board games, and a good laugh. I'm a very communicative person, so ask me anything. I speak English and Portuguese fluently, plus German and Spanish.
